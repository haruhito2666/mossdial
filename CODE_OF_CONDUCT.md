# Code of Conduct

People participating in Mossdial project spaces must make them welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

Be kind. Discuss ideas and code, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information. Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.

The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues, or other content that violates this policy. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel.
