# Code of Conduct

Mossdial contributors must make project spaces welcoming, respectful, and constructive.

Be kind. Discuss ideas and code, not people. Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, or publish another person's private information. Accept constructive feedback and take responsibility for mistakes.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits, issues, or contributions that violate this policy. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the repository's security or maintainer contact channel.
